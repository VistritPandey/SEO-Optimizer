# Local business search

Checks: local.identity, local.hours, local.schema, local.relevance. Sources: google-business-policy, google-local-ranking, google-local-schema, google-schema, google-spam in the [registry](../sources.json).

## Actual business identity

Establish verified real-world business identity, eligibility, actual locations/service area, contacts, hours and available services. Match the permitted location and current facts, not a guessed universal name/address/phone template. Do not create fake offices, add keywords to a legal/display name merely to manipulate results or invent locally available services.

Local Google results depend on relevance, distance and popularity. A code change cannot move a real business closer to a searcher. Avoid promising map positions or manufacturing many city pages with interchangeable copy. Location/service pages should reflect actual service coverage and distinct helpful details.

## Hours and structured data

Missing hours are unresolved if no authoritative hours are supplied. Do not assume 9–5, daily availability or a phone number from a nearby competitor. Match ordinary and exceptional schedules to current owner facts. Appointment-only behavior can be deliberate.

Use the specific applicable LocalBusiness subtype and visible real details for each location. Required and recommended rich-result properties differ; treat missing optional fields proportionately. Do not mark up self-serving LocalBusiness/Organization reviews as a route to Google review snippets, and never fabricate ratings. Valid JSON does not itself establish eligibility or actual display.

## External profiles

Repository access does not authorize a Business Profile or other listing change. Report a verified website/profile discrepancy as an external dependency until account access and project policy permit action. Unconfigured external listing access remains a recorded dependency. Repository automation never invents reviews or authorizes outreach.

Verify location facts, page content and schema consistently. When observing local outcomes, retain geography, query and time. Do not claim an onsite fix controls all external citations, reputation or distance.
